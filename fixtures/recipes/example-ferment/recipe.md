## Ingredients

- 500 g fictional test vegetable, sliced
- 1 liter water
- 30 g salt
- 2 cloves garlic

## Method

1. Dissolve the salt in the water to make a brine.
2. Pack the vegetable and garlic into a clean jar.
3. Pour the brine over, keeping everything submerged.
4. Seal loosely and leave at room temperature.
5. Each day, release built-up gas and taste for sourness.
6. On day 7, strain, bottle, and move to the fridge.
